Explore Q&A
How do I build a USMLE Step 1 study schedule?+

Choose the organ systems and foundational sciences you need, set their order, and select the resources you actually use. Then choose your daily study time and days off. step1tasks places tasks in your calendar so you can adjust the plan as you learn how long your work takes.

How long should my first read for Step 1 take?+

There is no single duration that fits every student. Your starting knowledge, remaining subjects, resources and weekly availability all matter. Use the task estimates as a starting point, compare them with your first week of actual study, and adjust. A reading schedule is not an assessment of exam readiness.

Can I choose my First Aid edition?+

Yes. Choose your edition in Settings under the resources you will use, then save your changes. Task references use available mappings for that edition. Missing or unverified mappings are labeled; an index page points to a topic and does not necessarily describe its full reading range. Check the reference against your own book.

Which resources can I organize in step1tasks?+

You can plan linked video lessons, First Aid reading, UWorld question practice and Mnemosyne flashcards. Select the resource types you use in Settings. step1tasks organizes references and study activity; it does not provide subscriptions or access to paid learning materials.

Should I study by organ system or foundational science?+

Start with the subjects that support your current course or your largest knowledge gaps. Foundational sciences can support later system-based learning, while organ systems help connect anatomy, physiology and pathology. You can choose your own sequence and edit it rather than follow a fixed order.

How should I plan a second or third read?+

Review what you have already completed and prioritize weak topics, retrieval practice and reviewing mistakes. Adjust your resource choices and available study hours to match that goal instead of repeating every first-read activity automatically. Use appropriate assessments and official exam guidance when deciding readiness.

Do I need an account to try the planner?+

Guests can explore feature previews and save a simple plan with one task per date and days off on the current browser. Clearing site data or switching devices removes access to that local plan. Creating an account requires an owner-issued access code; its selected features and duration determine which study tools you can use.

Does step1tasks include official USMLE questions or guarantee a result?+

No. step1tasks is an independent study planner. It does not issue official USMLE questions, determine eligibility or guarantee an exam outcome. Follow resource licensing terms and consult USMLE for current exam policies and requirements.
