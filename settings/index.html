    How the time estimates work

    Planning assumptions: reading 9 / 6 / 4 minutes per page for slow / average / fast readers; user-confirmed pages from your selected First Aid edition take precedence, otherwise estimates use source page volume. New / seen / familiar topics ×1.25 / 1 / 0.8; pass 1 / 2 / 3 ×1 / 0.72 / 0.55. Video runtime ÷ watch speed, plus 25% / 12% / 5% for pauses by pass. Questions allow 4 / 3 / 2.5 minutes each, including review. These are adjustable planning heuristics, not measured learning rates or promises of faster review.

    Reading, questions and flashcards use a planning range of 80–130% of the central estimate; videos use 90–120%. Ranges express planning uncertainty, not statistical confidence. Overlapping pages from the same edition are counted once within a task; pages encountered again in a later task remain part of that task's study session.
