Plan your study schedule

  1. Choose the topics you need for your current course or knowledge gaps.
  2. Select your First Aid edition and resources before building the calendar.
  3. Study the next unfinished resource, then mark it complete.
  4. Optionally rate your understanding and return to weaker topics later.

Task completion records work done, not mastery or exam readiness. First Aid index matches locate topics; missing mappings are labelled. Learning subscriptions and the book are separate from this planner.

How much time should I allocate?

Your familiarity, available days, reading pace and resource choices determine the workload. Use the estimates in your personal calendar as a starting point and compare them with actual study sessions. There is no fixed preparation duration that fits everyone.
