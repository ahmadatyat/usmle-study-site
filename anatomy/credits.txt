Adapted for Svitylo 3D Anatomy Atlas. Version 1.2.0 evaluates the Blender
geometry, converts axes and units, simplifies and packs each region.
https://www.npmjs.com/package/@authorod/svitylo-3d-anatomy-data/v/1.2.0

step1tasks selected 95 named thoracic structures, retained their registration,
merged rigid surfaces, reduced geometry in Blender, added vertex tints and
local occlusion, prepared two quantized meshopt GLBs, and rendered the posters and 36 scroll-sequence poses.
