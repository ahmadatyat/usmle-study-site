These student experiences are shared for educational benefit and peer support only. They are personal, self-reported opinions—not independently verified results, official guidance, medical advice, or a guarantee of passing. Preparation times, scores, resources, exam formats, scheduling rules, and policies may have changed. Historical numerical scores are not current passing thresholds. Do not use anecdotes to decide readiness or to take medication; consult qualified professionals when appropriate.
