    A STUDENT’S STEP 1 JOURNEY

    From medical school to a dedicated period

    OK
    Own KhreisatEnglish · 3 min read · Community archive

    A graduate’s account of building foundations and preparing for a June 2022 exam.

    PlanningResources
    Author-reported outcome: Pass. Not independently verified. Original language and wording are preserved; the title and introduction are editorial. Advice and exam policies may be outdated.

    Hello everyone.

    I am Own Khraisat (Non-US IMG) fresh graduate (graduated on 29th of may 2022), from Al- Balqa' Applied University (BAU) with GPA 3.16 and this is my USMLE Step 1 experience.

    SCORE: Pass (my exam was on 29th of June 2022)

    Resources:

    1. Initial period: From the 4th to 5th medical year. I do not consider this period as an actual preparation for step 1; it was just for gaining medical knowledge and building good basics from considerable resources. I did not have a specific schedule during this phase; I was just studying whenever I had free time after studying for the college and attending clinical rotations. I used the following resources:

    - Pathoma book and videos

    - First Aid

    - Boards and Beyond videos

    2. Dedicated period: From March 2021 to June 2022 (during my 6th medical year, I did not study for anything during this period except for Step 1) I did in this phase:

    - UW (1.5 times)

    - FA (about 2-3 times)

    - 100 cases of ethics for Conrad Fischer

    Self-Assessments: (arranged chronologically)

    - USWA1 (1st of march 2022): 172

    - NBME 30 (29th of march 2022): 192

    - NBME 29 mistakes (24th of June 2022): 203

    - UWSA2 (27th of June 2022): 196

    - New Free 120 (28th of June, 1 day before exam): 72%

    Exam day:

    1. I was so nervous at the beginning of the exam (which is normal), but I felt Okay after doing some questions which appeared not difficult.

    2. The time was not a problem at all. I was finishing the 40 questions in about 50 minutes, So I had about 10 minutes in each block for reviewing marked questions.

    3. I was marking about 15-20 questions per block except the 1st block which appeared more difficult for me than the other blocks, So I marked about 23 questions in this block.

    My thoughts:

    1. I did not use Anki. I tried it for one day but it was boring for me to just read decks.

    2. Every experience is personal, and what worked for me may not work for you and vice versa. That’s why I think that the best way to know something is to try it by yourself and then decide whether or not you will use this resource. For example, most people say Sketchy Microbiology is useful but I tried it and it was not useful for me.

    3. I tried to write my experience as concise as possible so do not hesitate to ask me if you have any questions.

    نصيحة لكل شخص (لو يرجع في الزمن هيك بسوي):

    1. بدرس كل سيستم او تشابتر من اي فيديوهات بس بنصح B&B ومباشرة بحل Uworld على اللي درستو.

    2. الامتحان ما بدو كل الوقت اللي انا اخذتو، انا كثير كنت اقطع دراسة واخذ بريكات بالاشهر وهذا اشي غلط وبخليك تنسى شو درست، يعني فعليا سنة كافية انك تقدم وتنجح.

    3. الامتحان اسهل من اي Self assessment رح تقدمو وغير هيك جو الامتحان كثير غير ورح تكون مركز.

    4. اللي ناوي يقدم الفترة الجاي الامتحان كثير بشبه ال new free 120 مع انو ما اجاني منهم بس نفس طول السؤال ونفس طريقة السؤال.

    5. ما توخدو نوتات من ال Uworld
