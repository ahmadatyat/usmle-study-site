How your plan is saved.

Your checklists, notes, and personal First Aid references are saved in this browser. Use the local profile menu above the task list to keep a separate plan for each learner on this device.

Local study profiles are not private accounts. Anyone using this browser profile can view these plans. They do not sync across devices. Export your plan as Markdown for a backup or to add it to your Obsidian vault.

Online accounts and cloud syncing are not connected yet.
